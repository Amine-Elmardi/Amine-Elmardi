FI-L2-004 · L2

Customer invoice remains open after payment

Problem

A received payment and invoice are still displayed as open items.

Error message / number

Exact error text and number not provided.

Root cause

Different assignment or reference numbers prevented automatic grouping.

Affected modules

FI-AR

Transactions

F-32, FBL5N

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Review invoice and payment in FBL5N
  2. Confirm payment reference with collections

Solution

  1. Match and clear the verified corresponding open items in F-32

Validation / configuration

Verify the invoice and payment clearing status in FBL5N.

Classification

L2: Functional analysis.
