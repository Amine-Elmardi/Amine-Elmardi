FI-L2-009 · L2

Receivables ageing total differs from reconciliation account

Problem

The AR ageing report does not agree with the G/L reconciliation balance.

Error message / number

Exact error text and number not provided.

Root cause

A direct journal entry to the reconciliation account bypassed the customer subledger in the scenario.

Affected modules

FI-AR / FI-GL

Transactions

FBL3N, FBL5N

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Compare customer items in FBL5N with account items in FBL3N
  2. Identify the unmatched direct posting

Solution

  1. Coordinate authorized reversal and reposting through the customer account

Validation / configuration

Repeat AR-to-GL reconciliation and confirm the difference is resolved.

Classification

L2: Functional analysis.
