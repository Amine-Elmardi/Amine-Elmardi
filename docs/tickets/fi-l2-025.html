FI-L2-025 · L2

Purchase order remains open after supplier payment

Problem

A supplier has been paid but the associated purchase order remains open.

Error message / number

Exact error text and number not provided.

Root cause

Invoice receipt is missing or unresolved; supplier payment clearing does not itself close the PO item.

Affected modules

FI-AP / MM

Transactions

ME23N, MIRO, MB5S

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Inspect PO history in ME23N and compare goods and invoice receipts
  2. Review GR/IR balances.

Solution

  1. Process the outstanding invoice or clearing only if justified and approved
  2. Reconcile the PO account assignment.

Validation / configuration

Review the updated PO history and MB5S.

Classification

L2: Functional analysis.
