FI-L2-044 · L2

Material ledger closing step fails

Problem

Actual costing cannot complete a specific closing step.

Error message / number

Exact error text and number not provided.

Root cause

The scenario identifies inconsistent material costing status or price determination.

Affected modules

CO-PC / MM / Material Ledger

Transactions

CKMLCP, CKM3, MM03

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Inspect CKMLCP log and identify material
  2. Review settings in MM03 and valuation in CKM3.

Solution

  1. Coordinate correction of the confirmed inconsistency
  2. Repeat the failed step after authorization.

Validation / configuration

Check closing log and valuation; do not alter price settings solely to bypass an error.

Classification

L2: Functional analysis.
