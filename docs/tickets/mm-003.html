MM-L2-052 · L2

Supplier special stock remains after subcontracting

Problem

A user reports remaining supplier stock a month after completing the subcontracting cycle and MIGO postings.

Error message / number

Not specified in the source.

Root cause

The source attributes the balance to an omitted movement 542, but uses the terms consignment and subcontracting interchangeably. The actual stock type is therefore not established by the document.

Affected modules

MM

Transactions

MB1B (542), MMBE, MB52

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Identify material, plant, storage location, supplier and remaining quantity
  2. Inspect supplier special stock in MMBE and MB52
  3. Clarify the process and stock type before any posting

Solution

  1. The source proposes MB1B with movement 542 and the material, supplier and quantity
  2. Because the source does not consistently identify the stock type, treat this as an unverified proposed correction and do not execute until the responsible team confirms its applicability

Validation / configuration

Check MMBE and MB52 after any approved correction. No configuration change is described.

Classification

L2: Functional analysis and coordinated correction.
