MM-L2-017 · L2

Goods receipt rejected because delivery exceeds tolerance

Problem

The warehouse attempts to receive more than the quantity ordered. MIGO rejects the posting instead of recording the excess quantity.

Error message / number

Not specified in the source.

Root cause

The purchase order item has an overdelivery tolerance of 0%. Even a small excess over the ordered quantity is therefore rejected in this scenario.

Affected modules

MM – Purchasing / Inventory Management

Transactions

ME22N, MIGO, ME11

Diagnosis

  1. Open the affected purchase order in ME22N.
  2. Select the item and inspect the Delivery tab.
  3. Check the overdelivery tolerance against the actual excess quantity.

Solution

  1. If the business permits the additional quantity, increase the item tolerance (the source gives 10% as an example) or use the unlimited-delivery indicator if appropriate.
  2. Save the purchase order.
  3. Retry the goods receipt in MIGO.

Validation / configuration

Check that the intended receipt is accepted. For a broader requirement, the source mentions the material master or purchasing info record and references OMGZ. The example percentage is not a universal recommendation.

Classification

L2: the support decision concerns a purchasing tolerance and an inventory posting.
