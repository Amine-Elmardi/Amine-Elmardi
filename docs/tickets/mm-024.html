MM-024 · L2

Invoice blocked due to mismatch with goods receipt quantity

Problem

During invoice verification in MIRO, the invoiced quantity differs from the quantity actually received. The discrepancy causes the invoice to be blocked in the documented scenario.

Error message / number

Not specified in the source.

Root cause

Goods-receipt-based invoice verification is active for the order item. The supplier has invoiced a greater quantity than the recorded receipt.

Affected modules

MM – Inventory Management / Invoice Verification; FI – GR/IR

Transactions

MIRO, MB03, MIGO, MR11, ME23N, ME11

Diagnosis

  1. In MIRO inspect the PO and goods receipt reference.
  2. Identify the relevant receipt documents in MB03.
  3. Determine whether a real receipt is still awaiting posting or the supplier invoice contains an incorrect quantity.

Solution

  1. If an actual receipt is outstanding in the system, post it in MIGO using movement 101.
  2. If the supplier invoiced the wrong quantity, request a corrected invoice.
  3. The source also describes adjusting the MIRO quantity to the received quantity and posting a partial invoice.

Validation / configuration

After resolution, monitor GR/IR using MR11. The source locates GR-based invoice verification at order-item or info-record level and mentions ME11.

Classification

L2: reconciliation of purchasing, goods receipt and invoice documents.

Source

Kandala Srinivasulu, SAP MM Support Tickets, PDF p. 18, #024. Document-based educational scenario; not independently verified.
