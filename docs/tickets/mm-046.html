MM-L3-008 · L3

Batch expiry and remaining shelf life are not controlled

Problem

Warehouse and quality teams need to prevent use of expired batches and monitor shelf life at receipt.

Error message / number

Not specified in the source.

Root cause

Batch management is active, but expiry checks or related settings are missing according to the source.

Affected modules

MM / WM

Transactions

MM02, MBC1, MB56, MIGO, LS51

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Review minimum remaining and total shelf life in MM02
  2. Examine expiry checking and the documented batch search strategy

Solution

  1. Maintain shelf-life data in MM02
  2. Activate the documented expiry check in SPRO
  3. Configure the described FEFO settings in MBC1 and LS51 where applicable

Validation / configuration

Test receipt and expiry handling in MIGO. Monitor dates through MB56. Confirm the intended blocking behavior before productive use.

Classification

L3: Configuration or enhancement.

Training case adapted from learning material; not independently validated in an SAP system. Production changes require authorization.
