MM-L3-033 · L3

Shelf life is not checked at goods receipt

Problem

Warehouse staff receive expired or near-expired batches without the desired minimum shelf-life check.

Error message / number

Not specified in the source.

Root cause

The source identifies missing plant-level activation or missing shelf-life data in the material master.

Affected modules

MM / QM

Transactions

MM02, MIGO

Diagnosis / requirements

  1. Confirm minimum remaining shelf life with QA
  2. Review total and minimum remaining shelf life in MM02 and the plant setting

Solution

  1. Maintain agreed shelf-life values in days in the material master
  2. Activate the documented shelf-life expiration check in SPRO
  3. Test MIGO with a production date or expiry date and an insufficient remaining shelf life

Validation / configuration

Confirm expected prompt and rejection behavior with QA before productive use. The source describes MM goods receipt expiry checking.

Classification

L3: Configuration or enhancement.

Training case adapted from learning material; not independently validated in an SAP system. Production changes require authorization.
