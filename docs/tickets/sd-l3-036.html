SD-L3-036 · L3

Third-party sales cost and revenue do not reconcile

Problem

Profitability reporting shows unexplained margins in direct supplier sales.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

Vendor invoice cost, statistical receipt or account assignment may differ from the intended flow.

Affected modules

SD / MM / FI / CO Third-party

Transactions

VA03, ME53N, ME23N, MIR4, MIRO, VF03, FBL5N, FBL1N

Diagnosis

  1. Trace order to requisition, PO history and supplier invoice
  2. Compare customer billing and FI/CO cost and revenue lines
  3. Confirm intended statistical receipt behavior.

Solution

  1. Coordinate confirmed process or account assignment correction with MM, SD and FI/CO
  2. Correct historical postings only with finance approval.

Validation / configuration

Reconcile cost, revenue and margin for representative complete transactions.

Classification

L3: Configuration / enhancement.

Training case adapted from learning material; not independently validated in an SAP system. Transactions depend on release. Production changes require authorization.
