SD-L4-001 · L4

Audit finding: revenue recognized in incorrect fiscal year

Problem

Multiple projects have revenue recognized in the wrong reporting year.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

Recognition timing settings or manual overrides conflict with contractual accounting rules.

Affected modules

SD / FI / CO Revenue Recognition

Transactions

VF44, VF45, VF46, OVE2, OVE3, FB01

Diagnosis

  1. Identify affected billing and recognition entries by period
  2. Compare contract dates, recognition method and FI postings
  3. Confirm accounting treatment with FI and auditors.

Solution

  1. Correct approved recognition settings in the applicable release
  2. Coordinate authorized reversal/reposting or adjustment journals with FI, retaining audit evidence.

Validation / configuration

Reconcile corrected amounts by fiscal year and obtain finance approval;Establish recurring closing review.

Classification

L4: Major projects / critical cross-functional incidents.
