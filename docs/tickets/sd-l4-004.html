SD-L4-004 · L4

Critical pricing condition removed in production

Problem

New orders have zero prices after a production change, putting revenue at risk.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

A mandatory condition such as PR00 was removed from the active pricing procedure.

Affected modules

SD Pricing / FI / Change Management

Transactions

V/08, VA05, VK13, SE10, STMS

Diagnosis

  1. Escalate the incident and isolate affected order creation
  2. Compare V/08 and transport history with the approved baseline
  3. Identify open and already billed documents.

Solution

  1. Restore the approved procedure through tested emergency change and CAB authorization
  2. Reprice eligible open orders in controlled batches with an approved pricing type
  3. Agree invoice adjustments with FI separately.

Validation / configuration

Compare representative orders, condition records and billed revenue with the approved baseline.

Classification

L4: Major projects / critical cross-functional incidents.

Training case adapted from learning material; not independently validated in an SAP system. Transactions depend on release. Production changes require authorization.
