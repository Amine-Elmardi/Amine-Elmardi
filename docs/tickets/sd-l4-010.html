SD-L4-010 · L4

Incorrect GST billing has affected submitted tax return

Problem

Wrong tax codes were used during a reported period.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

Tax classifications or condition records may have driven incorrect determination.

Affected modules

SD / FI Tax / Compliance

Transactions

VF05, VF03, VF11, VF01, VK12, XD02, MM02

Diagnosis

  1. Identify affected invoices and exact tax treatment with tax specialists
  2. Compare declared amounts with FI tax lines
  3. Preserve original records.

Solution

  1. Correct verified master/condition defect
  2. Have tax and finance approve compliant invoice correction and amended reporting procedure for the jurisdiction
  3. Do not cancel documents indiscriminately.

Validation / configuration

Reconcile corrected invoices, tax balances and amendment evidence;Obtain specialist sign-off.

Classification

L4: Major projects / critical cross-functional incidents.
