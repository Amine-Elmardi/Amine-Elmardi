SD-L4-015 · L4

Mass missing profitability records undermine closing reports

Problem

Many billing documents have no expected CO-PA record.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

Accounting transfer, PA transfer structure or derivation may be incomplete.

Affected modules

SD / FI / CO-PA

Transactions

KE24, VF05, VF03, KEI1, KEDR, KE4ST, KEND

Diagnosis

  1. Compare billing and profitability records by relevant period and process, not raw counts alone
  2. Check FI transfer in VF03
  3. Trace PA mapping and derivation with CO.

Solution

  1. Correct confirmed transfer or derivation defect through approved change
  2. Determine appropriate historical reposting or realignment with CO, since realignment alone cannot create absent postings.

Validation / configuration

Reconcile expected revenue and costs by profitability segment and add closing completeness control.

Classification

L4: Major projects / critical cross-functional incidents.
