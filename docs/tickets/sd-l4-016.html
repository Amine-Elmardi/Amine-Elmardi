SD-L4-016 · L4

Month-end mass billing failure blocks financial closing

Problem

Hundreds of invoices cannot create accounting documents at closing.

Error message / number

Exact message and number not provided.

Root cause

A common account determination, posting period or numbering defect may block the batch.

Affected modules

SD Billing / FI / Basis

Transactions

SM37, VF06, VF05, VFX3, VKOA, OB52, VN01, FBL5N

Diagnosis

  1. Declare major incident with SD, FI and Basis
  2. Inspect SM37 and accounting transfer errors, grouping documents by actual message
  3. Determine successful documents before retrying.

Solution

  1. Correct only the confirmed common cause through approved change or authorized period/number range maintenance
  2. Reprocess failed documents in monitored small batches after checking their status.

Validation / configuration

Reconcile billing and FI documents, customer balances and exceptions before closing.

Classification

L4: Major projects / critical cross-functional incidents.
